Two switches before we start

Pilot works your Mac the way you would, so macOS asks you to allow it. You only do this once.

  • See the screenTurn on “Pilot” under Screen & System Audio Recording.
  • Click and typeTurn on “Pilot” under Accessibility.
  • Connect to ClaudeSign in with your Claude account.

After switching both on, restart Pilot so macOS lets it in.
